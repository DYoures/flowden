## 1. Research Objective

Evaluate how combining **high-albedo cool roof membranes** with **street-level vegetative canopy evapotranspiration** reduces peak summer ambient and surface temperatures across dense urban canyons, and identify where moisture trapping creates conflicting thermal behavior.
