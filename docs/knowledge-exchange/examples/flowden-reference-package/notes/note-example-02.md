# Thermal Performance Summary — Vegetative Canopy Evapotranspiration

## 1. Pedestrian-Level Latent Cooling

Unlike roof-level reflective retrofits (covered in [[fkf:note-example-01|Thermal Performance Summary (High-Albedo Cool Roof Membranes)]]), street-canyon tree canopies directly intercept shortwave radiation before it reaches asphalt and masonry facades while converting sensible heat into latent heat via transpiration.

> "Synthetic Benchmark Excerpt (`exc-canopy-lai-01`): In synthetic canyon simulations with aspect ratio H/W = 1.5, increasing crown Leaf Area Index (LAI) from 1.2 to 3.8 lowered pedestrian-level Mean Radiant Temperature (MRT) by 14.6 K at 14:00 local solar time."

## 2. Nocturnal Sky-View Factor Contraction

Dense crown closure reduces the canyon Sky-View Factor (SVF) below `0.35`, trapping outgoing longwave radiation after sunset. Consequently, while daytime pedestrian comfort improves sharply, nocturnal air temperature at 2 m elevation cools 1.1 K more slowly unless canopy spacing permits vertical ventilation channels.
