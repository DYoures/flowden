# Thermal Performance Summary — High-Albedo Cool Roof Membranes

## 1. Mechanism & Baseline Reflectance

High-albedo polymeric roof membranes reduce shortwave solar absorption at the building envelope boundary. In our synthetic demonstration dataset (`src-example-01`), newly installed membranes exhibit an initial solar reflectance of `0.82` and thermal emittance of `0.90`.

> "Synthetic Benchmark Excerpt (`exc-albedo-aging-01`): Across simulated 36-month particulate deposition cycles, unwashed thermoplastic membranes declined from 0.82 to 0.64 solar reflectance, reducing peak midday roof surface cooling from 18.4 K to 11.2 K."

## 2. Boundary Layer Condensation Tradeoff

While cool roofs lower daytime sensible heat flux into the upper urban boundary layer, reduced evening surface temperatures can drop below the local dew point in humid basins (`exc-albedo-moisture-02`), accelerating biological soiling unless hydrophobic topcoats are applied.
